Understanding EEG Preprocessing

What does each EEG preprocessing step actually do to the signal? Each module lets you switch a step on and off and see how the recording, its spectrum and an event-related potential (ERP) change. Work through the modules in order: they all use the same recording, from one participant doing a memory test.

modules

  • 00
    Raw EEG Why EEG needs cleaning, and a scroll through the recording before any cleaning.
  • 01
    Filtering What high-pass, low-pass and notch filters do to the recording and the ERP.
  • 02
    ICA Removing blinks and eye movements, and what happens if you remove brain signal.
  • 03
    Bad channels Dropping a faulty electrode, and why losing one channel barely moves the ERP.
  • 04
    Bad trials Rejecting trials spoiled by artefacts, and the trade-off with keeping enough trials.
  • 05
    Re-referencing How the choice of reference changes every channel, and what it leaves unchanged.
  • 06
    The whole pipeline Building your own pipeline and comparing it with a recommended one.

Everything runs inside your web browser, so each module can take a minute or two to load. If a page feels slow, close other applications and browser tabs, such as music players and video calls, then reload it.
